Full concept

A moon for the night, a sun for the day

No ring. As you fast, a disc fills with light, and full means you've reached your goal. It follows the real sunrise and sunset where you are: a moon from sunset to sunrise, a sun in between. At sunrise the moon fades into the sun, at sunset the sun fades back into the moon, and your progress carries straight on.

How it reads at a glance

Fuller means closer

From a thin sliver to a full disc, and full is 16 hours. Everyone already knows how to read a moon.

Moon or sun follows your sky

It uses today's real sunrise and sunset where you are. At sunrise the moon fades into the sun, at sunset it fades back, and the fill stays the same. The moment is marked on the timeline too.

The line below is time

A low, flat arc with 16 dots, one per hour, and your start and end times at each end. You can drag along it.

The big number is time left

"How long until I can eat?" is what people ask most, so it gets the biggest type. Time past your goal isn't scored.

A moon and sun that feel premium

The first 3D version looked like a sticker: cartoon rays, blotchy texture and a flat yellow glow. It's now restrained and physical, like Apple's own work: less decoration, and detail that comes from real light.

A real moon face

The seas, craters and Tycho's bright rays sit where they really are, so it's recognisably our moon. It never spins, it only rocks gently.

Light draws the phase

The shadow edge curves around the ball and craters near it cast tiny shadows. The dark side stays faintly visible, like earthshine.

A sun that glows, not a drawing

A clean gradient ball, a little darker at the rim, with a soft warm bloom. The cartoon rays are gone: at your goal the bloom swells instead.

A restrained palette

Warm greys for the moon, amber to cream for the sun, and no saturated pure yellow.

What to borrow from (Not Boring) Weather

Borrow

  • An object you can touch. Press and drag the moon or sun and it tilts, the light shifts with it, and it springs back when you let go.
  • Depth. A real 3D ball. The moon has its real seas and craters and rocks gently like the real one, the shadow edge wraps around the sphere, and the sun glows softly. Drag it and it rolls. The phase still reads at a glance.
  • Sound. A soft tick each hour, each a little different in pitch so it never feels mechanical, and a chime when you reach full.
  • Scrubbing through time. Drag along the timeline and each hour clicks into place with a tap.
  • Skins later. Collectible moon and sun skins are a gentle way to earn money, while the timer itself stays free.

Skip

  • A full 3D engine. They build whole scenes with low-poly models, and only on iPhone. We launch on Android, where many phones are mid-range, so one small shader draws a real 3D moon and sun: no engine, no models, nothing to download. Older phones get the flat version automatically.
  • The chunky retro-game look. It would clash with the clean Apple look. We put the fun in touch and motion, not in the styling.
  • Sound in silent mode. Their sounds play even with the mute switch on. Fasts often run at night, so Lightly follows the phone's silent setting.
  • Lots of numbers. A weather app has a lot of data to show. We need one big number: time left.

Sources: App Store listing, TapSmart review, RevenueCat interview with Andy Allen, notbor.ing. None of them describe exactly how they draw the sun and moon.

What I think of the idea

I like it a lot. A moon phase is a progress bar everyone can already read, and it says "filling up" instead of a ring's "still missing". Most night fasts pass in your sleep, so a moon fits.

I gave the moon and the sun the same filling shape, so you read them the same way. In the eating window it slowly wanes, so a whole day is one full cycle.

Following the real sunrise and sunset means it changes with the seasons and is right anywhere. A fast that crosses sunrise turns from moon to sun partway, and progress never jumps. One thing to watch: location is private. We only need a rough city, so the app can start from your time zone's city and only ask for your precise location if you want it.
