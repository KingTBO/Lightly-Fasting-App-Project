Privacy Policy

Lightly (轻伴) · Draft of 2 November 2026

Draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyerThis is a first version written from what the app really does today. It is not legal advice and it is not final. Highlighted [bracketed] items still have to be filled in, and a lawyer should review the whole text before Lightly is released in an app store.

The short version

  • Lightly works without an account. Your fasts, plan and settings are saved on your phone.
  • If you choose to sign in, Lightly backs up your fasts and settings so they follow you to a new phone. That backup is the only thing we keep on a server.
  • Your precise location never leaves your phone. It is used only to work out your local sunrise and sunset.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling or sharing your data. Lightly does not ask for your weight and does not log food.
  • You can export your fasts, erase them from your phone, or delete your account and backup yourself, any time.

1. Who is responsible

Lightly (English) and 轻伴 (Chinese) is made by [legal entity name and address] (“we”, “us”). We decide how your data is used, so we are the “controller” of it. To contact us about privacy, write to [privacy contact email].

2. What stays on your phone only

Without an account, everything Lightly knows stays in your phone’s own app storage and is never sent to us:

  • your fasts (start and end times), current fast, plan and goal, cheat days, time off and streak;
  • your settings (language, appearance, sound, vibration, reminders) and usual start time;
  • your profile picture, if you pick one from your photos (a small 256 pixel copy; the original is not kept);
  • your answers to the health questions at the start, which turn Care mode on (see “Health-related information” below);
  • if you choose to answer them, your age, weight, height and two eating-habit answers, used only for a rough 30-day estimate (see “Health-related information” below);
  • your location, if you allow it: your phone shares it so the app can work out your local sunrise and sunset. Lightly rounds it to about one kilometre, keeps it on the phone, and never uploads it, not even in a backup. If you prefer, choose a nearby city instead.

Goal reminders are scheduled on your phone. They do not use a push service and nothing about them is sent to us.

Signing in is optional. When you do, the following reaches our server:

4. Health-related information

A list of when you fasted can say something about your health habits. Lightly also asks a few safety questions when you start (whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding, take diabetes medication, or have had an eating disorder). Your answers are saved (which of the three applied), so Lightly can turn on Care mode for you: no long fasts, no streaks, and shorter plans. You can review or change them in Me, Health answers. Lightly can also ask, only if you want, your age, weight and height and two questions about how you eat, to show a rough 30-day estimate. They are never used for anything else, never shown to anyone else, and not asked at all when Care mode is on. Once a month, in your monthly recap, Lightly may offer to note your weight again (optional); those entries are kept the same way. You can remove everything any time in Me, Weight and height. If you use “I feel unwell”, the date is kept so the pause can end. These items are on your phone and, if you sign in, in your backup.

We use this information only to run the app for you and to back it up. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it. Because it is health-related, [lawyer to confirm: if you are in the EU or UK, we ask for your explicit consent when you turn on the cloud backup, and you can withdraw it any time by deleting your account].

Lightly is not a medical product (see the Terms of Use). It does not diagnose or treat anything.

5. What we do not collect

  • No weight, body measurements, food logs or photos of food.
  • No precise location, contacts, microphone, camera stream or files other than a photo you choose yourself.
  • No advertising ID, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, no tracking cookies, no payments.

Lightly saves its settings in your browser’s or phone’s app storage. That storage is needed to run the app and is not used to follow you around.

6. Who else handles your data

We use a small number of service providers, only to run Lightly. They may process data only on our instructions.

  • Supabase stores the account and the backup and sends the sign-in email. Our project runs in Ireland (Amazon Web Services, eu-west-1).
  • Google, only if you choose “Continue with Google”. The app also loads your Google profile photo from Google’s servers. Google’s own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
  • GitHub hosts the Lightly web app and these pages. GitHub receives your IP address when you open them.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not give it to anyone else unless the law requires it.

7. Where your data is processed

The backup is stored in Ireland. If you use Lightly from outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States or the United Kingdom, your data travels to Ireland and may be accessed by our providers in other countries. [lawyer to confirm the transfer safeguards for each region]

8. How long we keep it, and how to delete it

  • Delete account (Me tab, then Delete account, or the web page) deletes your account, your backup and your profile picture from our server right away. It cannot be undone. Your phone keeps its own copy until you erase it with Erase everything.
  • While you keep an account, we keep the backup so you can restore it. We do not keep it for any other purpose.
  • Our hosting provider may keep encrypted system backups of the database for a short time after a deletion, [confirm retention period with the Supabase plan, for example up to 7 days], after which the data is gone from them as well.
  • Technical logs are kept for a short time, [confirm, for example up to 30 days].

9. Your rights

You are in control of your data. You can export your fasts from the Me tab, or download your backup from the delete-account page, correct things in the app, erase your phone’s data, and delete your account. You can also write to [privacy contact email] to ask us anything about your data.

If you are in the EU, UK or EEA

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your data, to receive it in a portable form, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority. We use your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), to keep it secure (legitimate interest), and, for health-related information, with your consent. [lawyer to confirm the legal bases]

If you are in California or another US state

You may ask to know, correct or delete your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for doing so. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context advertising.

10. Children

Lightly is for adults, 18 and over. Fasting is not suitable for children and teenagers. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you think a child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each backup is protected so that only the signed-in owner can read or change it. Lightly never keeps administrator keys in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if something happens that affects you we will tell you as the law requires.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect, and we will update the date at the top.

2. 只保存在手机上的内容

不登录时，轻伴知道的一切都保存在手机的应用存储里，不会发送给我们：

  • 你的断食记录（开始和结束时间）、进行中的断食、计划和目标、弹性日、休息时间和连续天数；
  • 你的设置（语言、外观、声音、振动、提醒）和通常开始时间；
  • 如果你从相册选了头像，会保存一份 256 像素的小图（不保留原图）；
  • 你在开始时对健康问题的回答，它们会开启关怀模式（见下文“健康相关信息”）；
  • 如果你愿意回答，还有你的年龄、体重、身高和两个饮食习惯的回答，只用于粗略估算 30 天（见下文“健康相关信息”）；
  • 你的位置（如果你允许）：手机把位置交给应用，用来计算当地的日出日落。轻伴会把位置取整到约一公里，只保存在手机上，绝不上传，备份里也不包含。你也可以改选附近的城市。

目标提醒是在你的手机上安排的，不使用推送服务，也不会把任何信息发给我们。

5. 我们不收集什么

  • 不收集体重、身体数据、饮食记录或食物照片。
  • 不收集精确位置、通讯录、麦克风、相机画面，也不读取文件，除了你自己选择的一张照片。
  • 没有广告标识符，没有统计分析或崩溃上报工具，没有追踪 Cookie，没有支付。

轻伴把设置保存在浏览器或手机的应用存储中。这些存储是应用运行所必需的，不会用来追踪你。
