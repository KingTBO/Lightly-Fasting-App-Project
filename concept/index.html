A fast that follows the sky

No ring. As you fast, a disc fills with light, and full means you've reached your goal. It follows the real sunrise and sunset where you are: a moon from sunset to sunrise, a sun in between. At sunrise the moon fades into the sun, at sunset the sun fades back into the moon, and your progress carries straight on.

How it reads at a glance

  1. Fuller means closer

    The light starts at the top and fills counter-clockwise, and full is your goal. You can read it at a glance.

  2. Moon or sun follows your sky

    It uses today's real sunrise and sunset where you are. At sunrise the moon fades into the sun, at sunset it fades back, and the fill stays the same. The moment is marked on the timeline too.

  3. The line below is time

    A low, flat arc with one dot per hour of your plan, and your start and end times at each end. You can drag along it.

  4. The big number is time left

    "How long until I can eat?" is what people ask most, so it gets the biggest type.

Your plan is your finish line

Whatever plan you pick, the disc is exactly full at your goal, and the dots on the timeline match your hours. Time past the goal isn't scored, and longer isn't treated as better. Four plans cover most people, so only those show up front. Everything else is one tap away under More plans. Tap a card to load it into the demo above.

New fasters start with 13:11 only; 16:8 opens after three completed fasts, then each plan after three at the one below. People who have fasted before get every everyday plan from the start. The 24 h and 36 h fasts start locked and unlock as you complete fasts, like a goal to work towards, not a paywall. A yes on the safety question means they're never offered, and Lightly suggests seeing a doctor. Lightly suggests the next step up (13, 16, 18) only after a week of comfortable fasts, and never pushes past the plan you chose.

What Lightly set up

    Playful, never pushy

    The game is there to get you through the hard hours, never to keep you hungry for longer.

    Your goal is the finish line

    Nothing is rewarded past your plan.

    A cheat day every week

    Office catch-up or a friend’s birthday? A cheat day keeps your streak. Hold up to two, use at most two in any 7 days. Ending early still counts.

    Feeling unwell ends it

    Answer "dizzy" or "unwell" in a check-in and Lightly suggests ending the fast, with no penalty.

    The timer stays free

    Timing, editing, history and the widget are free, and no paywall appears before your first fast.

    No food logging

    No calorie counting and no AI coach.

    Times you can fix

    Forgot to start? Log a fast afterwards, or drag the start and end times.

    New in the app

    All of this already works in the web app. Use the buttons to try it in the phone above.

    Bring your history with you

    Export from Zero or another app, preview it, then import. Duplicates are skipped, and the file never leaves your phone.

    A small celebration

    Reach your goal and hold to end: the disc blooms into sunrise. The same for every length.

    The tab bar steps aside

    Scroll down and the tab bar slides away for a full page. Scroll up and it returns.

    Plans open as you go

    New to fasting? Start with 13:11. A few fasts later 16:8 opens, then the rest one step at a time.

    Overtime is just time

    Keep going past your goal and it simply says how far over you are. Nothing extra is rewarded.

    Two timer styles

    Hero is the default, with very large light numerals. Classic is bolder. Both show hours, minutes and seconds, and you choose in Me.

    Only during Ramadan

    Ramadan, from dawn to sunset

    For one month the same sky timer keeps a sacred fast. The disc fills from dawn (Fajr) and is full at sunset (Maghrib), and it shows clearly when suhoor ends and when it's time for iftar. The whole mode turns quiet: no streaks, no games, no talk of weight. It appears on its own when Ramadan begins and leaves after Eid, so you won't find it in the app the rest of the year.     Times from your own sky

    Dawn uses the method you choose and sunset is your real local sunset. Nudge either one minute at a time to match your mosque's timetable.

    Calm, not a game

    No streaks, badges or challenges this month, and no talk of weight or fat burning. Just a count of days kept that only you see.

    No water reminders while fasting

    Nothing during the day asks you to drink or eat. Water reminders move to suhoor and after iftar.

    Days you don't fast, without guilt

    Travelling, unwell, on your period or any other reason: tap Not fasting today. It goes into a private count of days to make up.

    Health comes first

    If you have diabetes or take daily medication, talk to your doctor before Ramadan. Where days run very long (over 18 hours), Lightly shows a gentle health note.

    Break the fast together

    Share tonight's iftar time with family and friends, and everyone gets one gentle note at Maghrib. Off if you prefer.

    Worked out on your phone

    Prayer times are calculated on the phone. Your location never leaves it and is never sold.

    Kept apart from your plans

    Ramadan doesn't count towards long-fast unlocks. Your usual plan rests for the month and comes back after Eid.

    A companion, not an authority

    Lightly sits alongside your prayer app, never replaces it, and never rules on religious questions. We'll test the words and details with Muslim users before launch.

    Later

    The same approach fits the Bahá'í Nineteen-Day Fast (each March, sunrise to sunset) and Yom Kippur (each autumn, about 25 hours from sunset to nightfall the next day). Each would appear only in its own season and stay off unless you turn it on.

    Coming later · optional

    Fast with friends, when you want company

    A solo fast is the whole app, and the Today screen never asks you to invite anyone. When you want company, you will be able to send a friend one link.

    Friends will see whether you're fasting, eating or on a cheat day, and your streak. Never how many cheat days you've used, never your weight, and they can't change your plan.

    See it without opening the app

    One cross-platform app, Android first: an ongoing notification and a home-screen widget, then a Live Activity on iPhone. Google Play isn't in mainland China, so the Chinese launch needs Huawei, Xiaomi and other local stores.

    A moon and sun that feel premium

    The first 3D version looked like a sticker: cartoon rays, blotchy texture and a flat yellow glow. It's now restrained and physical, like Apple's own work: less decoration, and detail that comes from real light.

    A real moon face

    The seas, craters and Tycho's bright rays sit where they really are, so it's recognisably our moon. It never spins, it only rocks gently.

    Light draws the progress

    The lit part fills counter-clockwise from the top with a soft edge, and craters near it cast tiny shadows. The unlit side stays faintly visible, like earthshine.

    A sun that glows, not a drawing

    A clean gradient ball, a little darker at the rim, with a soft warm bloom. The cartoon rays are gone: at your goal the bloom swells instead.

    A restrained palette

    Warm greys for the moon, amber to cream for the sun, and no saturated pure yellow.
